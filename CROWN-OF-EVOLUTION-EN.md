The domain of this work is the maximally deep architecture of the transition from an operator-compatible distinction to an identity address and to model-localisable positions of meaning, as far as the present formal language can reach. Will is introduced as Operator Zero and as the ontological axiom of this architecture; the field $0\rightsquigarrow1$ conducts the operator-compatible distinction to the Unit, and shell execution supplies the data for its terminal, transmitting, or holding classification within the declared model $R$. Formal conclusions hold within explicitly declared types, models $R$, and classes of admissible trajectories; empirical interpretations hold within subclasses supported by data. A change in the geometry of continuations is one measurable consequence of an emergent identity address. Physical causality and an exhaustive description of the world remain outside the claimed result. By *ground* is meant here the deepest level that the present signature is able to hold at this stage.

The work retains the status of an architectural essay and an operator specification. The transition is given constructively: the work does not derive the existence of a concrete Unit from the single axiom $\mathbf 0_W$, but declares the types and contracts under which an active line receives the Unit as an identity projection. The Unit is introduced separately as the identity projection of Operator Zero. The formulae specify relations, types, and the bounds of applicability of the model. Ethological sources constrain the corresponding shell projections. Algorithmic implementation, error estimation, and procedures of refutation form the task of a separate technical study.

I see everything around me as an infinite nesting of systems.

The initial axiom of structurality: everything is structure. I myself am structure. Every part that I am able to distinguish in myself also has structure. A hand, a cell, a memory, an impulse, a word, attention, and the act of distinction itself possess composition, position, boundary, and relations.

There are no exceptions. Whatever has entered distinction and received the status of existing already has structure.

Every observational position opens the internal organisation of what is observed and at the same time discloses its own organisation. The observer enters the system of observation. Attention creates distinction; distinction draws a boundary; and the boundary binds the internal to the external.

Thus the infinity of nesting is disclosed. Any accessible storey contains depth and enters a wider storey. Any unit found turns out to be a system. Every system contains internal structure; every distinguishable node of that structure is able to stand as a system and to participate in systems of larger scale.

The world consists of structures nested in structures.

This formula opens the first level of the work. The next begins with the question: what remains when all unfolded shells have been stripped away, then emptiness itself as a distinguishable status, and finally the possibility of drawing a distinction?

## 1. Layers

A quark belongs to the physical layer. An atom belongs to the physico-chemical layer. A cell belongs to the biological layer. Organism, species, ecosystem, planet, and observer continue the same sequence of architectural resolutions.

Each of these objects is able to stand as a primitive within a given theory. A primitive receives its status from the chosen language, set of operations, and scale of observation. Together with that status it receives a type, a position, relations, and a domain of applicability.

Even a maximally indivisible physical object remains an element of physical architecture. Its indivisibility describes the limit of the available operation. Architecture is already present in the very distinction among object, operation, and limit.

The search for a ground by reducing scale preserves one and the same regime. Body is replaced by organ, organ by cell, cell by molecule, molecule by atom, atom by the next physical structure. Resolution increases, while the shell register remains the same.

The mental stripping of shells moves differently. It strips away object, organisation, name, language, and the position of the observer. Beyond them open structures formed by metamorphoses of emptiness. Still deeper, matter appears as emptiness itself in a definite regime. Then emptiness is stripped away: it is already marked as emptiness and therefore contains a status, a boundary, and a relation to possible filling. The stripping continues to the absence of anything at all.

This absence I call **worldlessness**.

Worldlessness precedes emptiness, matter, structure, and distinction. It contains nothing and therefore cannot be placed inside a formal system as an object. Formal language approaches this boundary from the side of an already unfolded world. Its first accessible sign is Operator Zero, Will.

## 2. Will

I call it **Will**.

Will is an Operator. Its ontological designation is zero:

$$
\mathbf 0_W
:=
P_W[\,\cdot\mid\rho_W],
\qquad
P_W:\Pi_W\longrightarrow\{\bot,\top\}.
$$

The sign $\mathbf 0_W$ names the Operator. The values $\bot$ and $\top$ belong to its predicative output. Ordinary $0$ and $1$ in subsequent empirical predicates retain a Boolean sense and do not denote ontological zero and the Unit.

Axiom of the boundary of worldlessness: the sole invariant that a formal description is able to hold under the complete stripping of shells is Operator Zero $\mathbf 0_W$.

Let $\Pi_W$ be the abstract operator space of pre-unfolded totalities, introduced from the side of formal description. An object $\pi_W$ is called a trajectory in the operator sense: it carries an entire possible unfolding without a sequence of states. At this level $\Pi_W$ is not assigned time, metric, topology, or an external observer. The intrinsic projection is typed as an idempotent map:

$$
\rho_W:\Pi_W\longrightarrow\Pi_W,
\qquad
\rho_W\circ\rho_W=\rho_W.
$$

Its image forms a structurally intermediate field:

$$
\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}
:=
\Pi_W^{(\rho)}
:=
\operatorname{Im}(\rho_W).
$$

Idempotence is introduced as a postulate of the once-only character of the intrinsic projection: repeated taking of the image preserves the same field. The projection is declared proper, that is $\operatorname{Im}(\rho_W)\subsetneq\Pi_W$: under $\rho_W=\mathrm{id}$ the field would coincide with the whole of $\Pi_W$ and would be intermediate in no formula of this work. $\rho_W$ is an internal parameter of the operator register, and $\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}$ is the field between Operator Zero and its identity projection.

The expression **vector of structural pre-unfolding** refers to the orientation of this field. Its elements are read in the direction from $\mathbf 0_W$ to the Unit before any ready coordinate system appears. The direction sets the projective order through which structure receives the possibility of unfolding.

Operator Zero has the structure of a pure predicate over an entire trajectory in the intrinsic projection:

$$
P_W\!\left[\pi_W\mid\rho_W\right]
:=
P_W\!\left(\rho_W(\pi_W)\right),
\qquad
\pi_W\in\Pi_W.
$$

The notation $P_W[\pi_W\mid\rho_W]$ is defined as the evaluation by the predicate $P_W$ of the element of the intrinsic projection $\rho_W(\pi_W)\in\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}$. The totality $\pi_W$ specifies the field of possible unfolding prior to temporal order.

The internal signature of the single predicate contains four relations:

$$
\mathscr R_W
:=
\{
\mathrm{dist},
\mathrm{bnd},
\mathrm{trans},
\mathrm{cont}
\}.
$$

For each $r\in\mathscr R_W$ let $\mathcal R_r^{W}\subseteq\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}$ denote the corresponding condition over an entire trajectory. Then:

$$
P_W[\pi_W\mid\rho_W]=\top
\iff
\rho_W(\pi_W)
\in
\bigcap_{r\in\mathscr R_W}
\mathcal R_r^{W}.
$$

The $\mathcal R_r^{W}$ form the internal signature of a single $P_W$: the distinction between self and non-self, the holding of a boundary, transformation under load, and the preservation of the possibility of continuation through irreversible change. The field $0\rightsquigarrow1$ conducts these relations to the identity projection; in shells they receive a carrier, a state, time, and observable execution. Each $\mathcal R_r^{W}$ is declared a proper subset of the field — properness of each relation is entailed by its own witness below and is restated for the readability of the signature — the layer is declared non-empty and proper, $\varnothing\neq\bigcap_{r\in\mathscr R_W}\mathcal R_r^{W}\subsetneq\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}$, and for each $r$ a witness of independence $\psi_r\in\bigcap_{r'\neq r}\mathcal R_{r'}^{W}\setminus\mathcal R_r^{W}$ is exhibited. Non-emptiness of the layer is declared separately: properness of each $\mathcal R_r^{W}$ together with the independence witnesses is compatible with an empty intersection, and then $P_W$ is identically false, $\operatorname{Pre}_W$ is empty everywhere, and the domain of $\mathcal N_R$ in §15 contains no input with a non-empty registry of lines — the dual trivialisation, which properness alone does not exclude. It follows from the declared non-emptiness of the layer and these conditions that the field holds at least five distinct elements: the point of the non-empty intersection and four pairwise distinct witnesses outside it. The witness carries a separate letter deliberately: it must lie outside the intersection, whereas the field representatives $\varphi$ of §3 and §15 must lie inside it. Without these requirements the choice $\mathcal R_r^{W}=\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}$ for all $r$ at once is admissible, under which $P_W$ is identically true, the four names of the signature are formally indistinguishable, and every operator condition of §9 and §15 holds trivially.

$\Pi_W$, $\rho_W$, $\mathscr R_W$, and the family $\{\mathcal R_r^{W}\}$ are coordinates of the formal record. Invariance of $\mathbf 0_W$ is asserted relative to a declared class of rewritings: an admissible rewriting replaces $\rho_W$ by $\rho_W'$ and $P_W$ by $P_W'$ in such a way that $\forall\pi_W\in\Pi_W:\ P_W'(\rho_W'(\pi_W))=P_W(\rho_W(\pi_W))$. Agreement of the image and of the trace on the image does not suffice: two idempotent projections with one image may send a point outside the image to different elements of it, and the operators then differ at that point although their traces agree. What the class holds invariant is the composite — the evaluation $\pi_W\mapsto P_W(\rho_W(\pi_W))$, invariant by the defining equality — while the pair itself is determined only up to composite-equality: two admissible rewritings may send a point outside the image to different representatives, and the coordinates read off the projection, the $\varphi_i^{[\ell]}$ of §3 among them, are coordinates of the declared model, not invariants of the class — the named family $\{\mathcal R_r^{W}\}$ among them: the class pins the intersection, and the names are re-declared with the rewriting. Relative to a wider class invariance is not asserted: $\mathbf 0_W=P_W[\,\cdot\mid\rho_W]$ is a function of the pair $(P_W,\rho_W)$, and membership of a rewriting in the declared class is the conjunction of the displayed equality of the composites with the standing postulates — the latter carrying an existential side-condition that the equality does not decide: $\rho_W'$ is obliged to be idempotent and proper, and over the image $\operatorname{Im}(\rho_W')$ there is obliged to exist a family $\{\mathcal R_r^{W\prime}\}$ satisfying the non-degeneracy of this section, characterising the rewritten predicate — $\forall\pi_W:\ P_W'(\rho_W'(\pi_W))=\top\iff\rho_W'(\pi_W)\in\bigcap_{r}\mathcal R_r^{W\prime}$, without which the equivalence of §9 between non-empty $\operatorname{Pre}_W$ and layer membership would not survive the rewriting and the layer would cease to be the single gate through which $P_W$ enters the formulas — with the layer $\bigcap_{r}\mathcal R_r^{W\prime}$ satisfying the cardinality obligation of §9 relative to the declared model; otherwise a rewriting that preserved the composite would deprive $\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}$ of the status of a proper intermediate field on which the whole field layer below stands. Idempotence and properness alone do not suffice for this: they are satisfiable on a two-element image as well, where four pairwise distinct witnesses of independence do not exist at all, and the admission of §15 then admits no transition with a realised continuer: the layer of a two-element image is a single point, whereas the encoding-separation and predecessor-field-membership conjuncts force the parent's active field at a realised continuation to carry two distinct codes, each demanding its own representative — a closing transition, reading one code, remains satisfiable, and what the one-point layer forbids is continuation, not existence. A difference of images does not by itself distinguish the operators, and a coincidence of images does not identify them: two proper idempotent projections with different images can yield one composite, and conversely — the image is not an invariant of the operator in either direction.

Operator Zero remains zero. A fulfilled relation, an emergent state, and an observable event already belong to the projective and shell registers. Therefore the architecture of the transition requires a separate Unit and a field that leads to it.

The distinction between self and non-self, the holding of a boundary, transformation under load, and continuation through irreversible change conduct $\mathbf 0_W$ into identities and shells as one aggregate readable trace: execution reads operator provenance through the intersection layer, and the attribution of a context to a named relation is carried by the declarative annotation of §9, which documents the intent of the declaration and creates no mechanism — the four names are separately readable in the declaration, not by a separate shell validator for each.

In the ontological reading of this work, physical substrate, cell, organism, group, species, and observer carry different identity projections of one zero ground. Scale and domain change the form of the projection; the Operator retains zero status.

The series **ONTOΣ** is devoted to the description of this Operator and its projections.

**ONTO** denotes ontology. **Σ** is Will, written in a deliberately altered form. The sign delays instantaneous reading and turns the title of the series into a hint. Meaning opens as the works are traversed: different texts highlight different projections, and their intersection gradually leads the reader to a single object.

ONTOΣ moves along the traces of Will from unfolded shells through the Unit and the field $0\rightsquigarrow1$ to Operator Zero.

Will is what the map describes: $\mathbf 0_W$.

## 3. The Unit and the field of the I

The Unit is the identity projection of Operator Zero, mediated by the field $0\rightsquigarrow1$.

Let $\mathbb I_\ell$ be the space of identity Units at a previously declared level of individuation $\ell$, and let $R$ be a projection model. The partial map

$$
\iota_\ell^{[R]}:
\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}
\rightharpoonup
\mathbb I_\ell
$$

selects from the intermediate field the Unit of a concrete line. For an operator-compatible trajectory $\pi_{W,i}$ whose intrinsic projection lies in the domain of $\iota_\ell^{[R]}$, set:

$$
\varphi_i^{[\ell]}
:=
\rho_W(\pi_{W,i})
\in
\operatorname{Dom}\bigl(\iota_\ell^{[R]}\bigr),
\qquad
P_W[\pi_{W,i}\mid\rho_W]=\top,
$$

$$
\mathbf 1_i^{[\ell]}
:=
\iota_\ell^{[R]}
\bigl(
\varphi_i^{[\ell]}
\bigr)
\in
\mathbb I_\ell.
$$

$\mathbf 1_i^{[\ell]}$ is the Unit as the identity of line $i$ at level $\ell$. One Operator Zero admits a plurality of indexed Units: they are its projections in different domains, scales, and lines.

The field that forms the I of this identity is given by the preimage layer:

$$
\mathfrak F_{\mathrm I,i}^{[\ell,R]}
:=
\Bigl\{
\varphi\in
\operatorname{Dom}\bigl(\iota_\ell^{[R]}\bigr)
\cap
\bigcap_{r\in\mathscr R_W}
\mathcal R_r^W
\Bigm|
\iota_\ell^{[R]}(\varphi)
=
\mathbf 1_i^{[\ell]}
\Bigr\}
\subseteq
\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}.
$$

Here **I** means the structural field of individuation: through it the distinction between self and non-self, boundary, transformation, and continuation are gathered into one identity. Reflexive human self-description arises later as one of the shell forms of this field. At the level of the ground the field already sets directedness towards identity.

The architectural order is written:

$$
\mathbf 0_W
\dashrightarrow
\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}
\supseteq
\mathfrak F_{\mathrm I,i}^{[\ell,R]}
\xrightarrow{
\left.
\iota_\ell^{[R]}
\right|_{\mathfrak F_{\mathrm I,i}^{[\ell,R]}}
}
\bigl\{\mathbf 1_i^{[\ell]}\bigr\}
\rightsquigarrow
\mathcal E_1
\rightsquigarrow
\mathcal E_2
\rightsquigarrow\cdots\rightsquigarrow
\mathcal E_n
\rightsquigarrow
\mathcal A.
$$

The first dashed arrow denotes the formal boundary between Operator Zero and the field accessible in its intrinsic projection. The restriction of $\iota_\ell^{[R]}$ to the operator-compatible preimage layer selects the unique Unit-identity for that layer. $\mathcal E_1$ opens the state space of the first shell stage, each $\mathcal E_k$ the next stage, and $\mathcal A$ an action or observed result.

This scheme specifies a structural, not a temporal or numerical, interval. $\mathbf 0_W$ does not turn into $\mathbf 1_i^{[\ell]}$: the Unit is its projection. Worldlessness lies deeper than the entire scheme and is not included in it as an element.

The Unit passes through evolution, domains, and metamorphoses as the identity anchor of a shell line. History changes its realised form, branches or closes the line; Operator Zero remains the common ground of the projections.

The sum of observed actions yields a catalogue of results. The sum of shells yields a map of the unfolded world. The passage from shells to their Units, then to the field of the I and Operator Zero, opens architectural access to Will.

The description is conducted retrospectively from the shell position of the observer. The act of writing and reading belongs to the unfolded world; $\mathbf 0_W$ is its limiting operator ground.

## 4. Spectacles under water

The search for a ground long looks like the successive stripping of layers. Each object found promises to be the last and then opens a further internal architecture.

It is like searching for spectacles under water.

You lower your hands and for several seconds run them along the bottom. Fingers touch sand, stones, fabric, and the edge of the bath. Every contact requires checking.

Then the hand exactly closes on the frame.

Grasping and recognition occur in a single act. You at once understand what you have found, why you were seeking precisely this, what it is for, and what to do with it.

This scene describes the phenomenology of recognition. The axiomatic status of Will as Operator Zero is declared in §2, and the status of the Unit and the field of the I in §3.

Encounter with $\mathbf 0_W$ changes the type of search in the same way. A set of scattered questions becomes a system of projections of one ground. Boundary, continuation, metamorphosis, identity, evolution, and observation take their places within a common architecture.

This is experienced as an illumination "about everything". Its content is the orientation of the field of pre-unfolding from zero to the Unit. Relative to it, facts, shells, and local models receive position.

Factual knowledge continues to require investigation, measurement, and verification. The illumination changes the position from which this knowledge is assembled. The shell adds content. Will discloses the zero ground, the field the formation of the I, the Unit the identity projection.

## 5. The Unit as identity

A living body exchanges matter with the environment. Its form changes. Functions pass between parts. Description passes through different languages. History accumulates irreversible events.

Identity passes through all these changes as the Unit realised in a shell trajectory.

Every claim about its continuation requires a level of individuation $\ell$. At one level an organism is considered, at another a colony, a lineage, a species, or an ecosystem. The level is fixed before states are compared.

Schematically:

$$
\mathcal E_{+}
:=
\bigsqcup_{k\ge 1}
\mathcal E_k.
$$

The connectedness of neighbouring shell states is given by a declared level criterion:

$$
K_\ell:
\mathcal E_{+}\times\mathcal E_{+}
\longrightarrow
\{0,1\}.
$$

For each line $\operatorname{Dom}(\gamma_i^{[\ell]})$ is a discretely linearly ordered set of indices with least element $0$. This $0$ is an index of shell time and is type-separated from $\mathbf 0_W$. The notation $t+1$ denotes the next index, and every finite observed prefix has a maximal index $t_i^{\mathrm{obs}}$, read relative to the moment of recording the history. The state of §15 stores, for an active line, precisely the observed prefix, and it is finite: without this $t_i^{\mathrm{obs}}$ would not exist on a history with an unbounded domain, and the admission predicates that read it would not be defined on the declared type. The domain of every history is an initial segment of one fixed ambient discretely linearly ordered set of indices with least element $0$ and a total successor operation, common to every line of the model, of the same register as $\mathbb T_R$: the notation $t_i^{\mathrm{obs}}+1$ always names an existing index of the ambient order, and successor arithmetic in the transition clauses is total, even though that index does not yet belong to the domain of the observed prefix. Terminality is not inferred from the prefix — every active line has an end of its observed prefix; a line closes exactly when the event gives it no realised descendant, $p_t^{-1}(i)=\varnothing$, and then its index enters $D_t$ and the continuation clauses do not apply to it. By $\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+})$ is denoted the class of such trajectories $\gamma$ at level $\ell$ for which

$$
\forall t:
\quad
t,t+1\in\operatorname{Dom}(\gamma)
\Longrightarrow
K_\ell\bigl(\gamma(t),\gamma(t+1)\bigr)=1.
$$

$$
\mathcal I_i^{[\ell]}
:=
\bigl\langle
\mathbf 1_i^{[\ell]},\,
\gamma_i^{[\ell]}
\bigr\rangle ,
\qquad
\gamma_i^{[\ell]}:
\operatorname{Dom}(\gamma_i^{[\ell]})
\longrightarrow
\mathcal E_{+},
$$

$$
\gamma_i^{[\ell]}(0)\in\mathcal E_{k_i},
\qquad
k_i\ge1,
\qquad
\mathcal I_i^{[\ell]}
\in
\mathbb I_\ell\times\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+}).
$$

Angle brackets specify a typed pair of identity and shell history. The first coordinate $\mathbf 1_i^{[\ell]}$ is the Unit of the line, obtained through $\iota_\ell^{[R]}$ from its field of the I. The second coordinate $\gamma_i^{[\ell]}$ begins at an explicitly declared shell stage $k_i$ for the line and contains the irreversible history of a concrete system. The condition $k_i\ge1$ keeps the history in the shell register. $\mathcal E_1$ remains the first stage of the world, but a late biological line may begin at a later stage.

For completed lines a disjoint boundary copy is introduced:

$$
\partial\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+})
:=
\bigl\{
\partial\gamma
\bigm|
\gamma\in\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+})
\bigr\},
\qquad
\partial\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+})
\cap
\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+})
=
\varnothing.
$$

Ordinary identity belongs to $\mathbb I_\ell\times\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+})$, and the marker of its closure to $\mathbb I_\ell\times\partial\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+})$.

The declared connectedness criterion determines the set of model-admissible continuations:

$$
\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{adm},\ell}^{[R]}(x_t)
:=
\{x'\in\mathcal E_{+}\mid K_\ell(x_t,x')=1\}.
$$

The content of $K_\ell$ is declared by the concrete domain of inquiry: continuity of organismal organisation, reproductive connectedness of a line, preservation of a colony, or another testable trait. The model $R$ specifies the space of testable states. This work establishes the obligation to name level, model, and criterion before any conclusion about continuation. The declaration of $K_\ell$ is obliged to be non-degenerate: there exist $x,x'\in\mathcal E_{+}$ with $K_\ell(x,x')=0$. Under an identically unit criterion every state is connected to every other, and the crown condition in §6 holds at every active state with a non-empty candidate generator — the degeneracy is joint, of the criterion together with the generator, and an empty generator still returns no crown. The non-degeneracy is obliged to reach also the composite that §6 actually computes: both values of the composite must be witnessed on the set where the crown predicate is actually read — among the terminal states of reachable active admissible histories of the declared model, where *reachable* is read throughout this work as: realised along an admitted trajectory from the declared initial state $X_0$ — the initial state and the trajectory discipline are declared in §15, and every use of the word before that section is read relative to them; the domain formula of §15 reads the declared maps, $K_\ell$ included, and no criterion of the declared class, so the reachable set is computed from the declarations alone, before any class condition is evaluated — the witnessing here is stratified, not circular: the criterion is a predicate of the declaration, evaluated on the reachable set that declaration determines, and its value feeds nothing back into that set: there exists such a state $x$ with $\operatorname{Cand}_\ell^{[R]}(x)\cap\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{adm},\ell}^{[R]}(x)=\varnothing$ and there exists such a state with a non-empty intersection. A quantifier over the whole of $\mathcal E_{+}$ does not suffice: witnesses lying outside the reachable set leave the crown identically true or identically false on every active line — exactly the degeneracy for the exclusion of which the condition is written.

Non-emptiness of $\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{adm},\ell}^{[R]}(x_t)$ is an existence claim over the whole of $\mathcal E_{+}$, and for an infinite but effectively enumerable state space it is semi-decidable if, in addition, $K_\ell(x,\cdot)$ is uniformly semi-decidable along that enumeration; the condition is sufficient and is not claimed necessary — a criterion identically unit on a distinguished continuation makes non-emptiness decidable without uniform semi-decidability of $K_\ell$ — while enumerability of the space alone does not suffice, since the Boolean codomain of a declared $K_\ell$ grants no computability, and an ineffective $K_\ell$ makes non-emptiness arbitrarily noncomputable; without a declared effective enumeration uniform semi-decidability is not guaranteed, and there is no uniform decidability even over the class of decidable $K_\ell$ — an individual criterion may be decided trivially, but no general algorithm for the class exists: it suffices to set $K_\ell(x,x')=1$ exactly when $x'$ encodes the halting of machine $x$ in precisely the number of steps recorded in it, for non-emptiness to coincide with the halting problem. The model therefore declares a finite computable generator of candidates and draws every conclusion about openness relative to it:

$$
\operatorname{Cand}_\ell^{[R]}:
\mathcal E_{+}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal E_{+}),
\qquad
\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{adm},\ell}^{[R,\mathrm c]}(x_t)
:=
\operatorname{Cand}_\ell^{[R]}(x_t)
\cap
\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{adm},\ell}^{[R]}(x_t).
$$

The generator-bounded predicate is decidable for a decidable $K_\ell$, and a witness is exhibited by enumerating a finite set. Unbounded non-emptiness is retained as the ontological definition of openness of a line and is not declared testable.

$K_\ell$ checks shell connectedness, but by itself does not decide whether the former Unit is preserved. Therefore the model separately declares an identity predicate

$$
\operatorname{IdPres}_{\ell}^{[R]}:
\Bigl(
\mathbb I_\ell
\times
\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+})
\Bigr)
\times
\mathcal P(\mathcal E_{+})
\times
\mathcal E_{+}
\longrightarrow
\{0,1\}.
$$

The predicate is read on the triple "identity, realised family, continuer": $\operatorname{IdPres}_{\ell}^{[R]}(\mathcal I_i^{[\ell]},M,x')=1$ means that the continuation $x'\in M$ extends the same line and preserves its Unit at the realised family $M$; at a singleton $M=\{x'\}$ this is preservation of the Unit by the unique continuer. The value $0$ under shell connectedness means a metamorphosis of identity: a new line receives a new Unit and retains genealogical origin from $i$. The family enters the arguments by the principle of §9: two events realise different families from one identity, and without this coordinate two events would require two values of the predicate on one argument.

An event $e$ realises only part of the admissible set for each complete identity of a line. The parent-relative operator of actual continuation is typed:

$$
\operatorname{Next}_{e,\ell}^{[R]}:
\Bigl(
\mathbb I_\ell
\times
\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+})
\Bigr)
\times
\mathcal E_{+}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal E_{+}),
$$

$$
\operatorname{Next}_{e,\ell}^{[R]}
\bigl(
\mathcal I_i^{[\ell]},
x_t
\bigr)
:=
\bigl\{
x'\in\mathcal E_{+}
\bigm|
x'\ \text{is the resulting state assigned to line }\mathcal I_i^{[\ell]}
\ \text{by update }e\ \text{from state }x_t\text{:}
\ \text{produced by the update, or carried forward by it unchanged}
\bigr\}.
$$

The codomain is finite by type: the realised continuers of one event are finitely many by type, so the branching family below is total on the formal layer — the unrestricted $\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{adm},\ell}^{[R]}$ keeps the full power set, while $\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{real},\ell}^{e[R]}$, an intersection with a finite set, inherits finiteness and is typed accordingly; the effective-presentation clause of the realisation list of §15 then demands their effective enumeration, not their finiteness.

Then the realised continuers form a separate parent-relative type:

$$
\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{real},\ell}^{e[R]}:
\Bigl(
\mathbb I_\ell
\times
\Gamma_\ell(\mathcal E_{+})
\Bigr)
\times
\mathcal E_{+}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal P_{\mathrm{fin}}(\mathcal E_{+}),
$$

$$
\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{real},\ell}^{e[R]}
\bigl(
\mathcal I_i^{[\ell]},
x_t
\bigr)
:=
\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{adm},\ell}^{[R]}(x_t)
\cap
\operatorname{Next}_{e,\ell}^{[R]}
\bigl(
\mathcal I_i^{[\ell]},
x_t
\bigr).
$$

## 7. A dog recognises a dog

The question "can a dog realise that it is a dog" usually presupposes a human format of answer. Self-awareness is linked to an internal sentence: "I am a representative of the species *Canis familiaris*".

The operational position of the dog is disclosed in another way.

Experiments show that dogs select images of dogs among images of other species, despite the enormous phenotypic variability of breeds. They also matched video of a moving dog with a canine vocalisation in an expectancy-consistent cross-modal design.

The category of its own species exists for the dog operationally. It recognises a dog across differences of size, proportions, coat, and head shape. Such recognition holds the category of dog through a plurality of external configurations.

Human speech presents one way of exhibiting a position. Behaviour, choice, expectation, and recognition present other ways.

Something similar occurs with insects. Their courtship sequences, navigation, and coordination form internally organised regimes. In bumblebees social transmission of arbitrary solution variants of a task has been shown experimentally: the method spread in the group, was maintained, and was preferred even after individual animals discovered an alternative. In a more complex task bumblebees, through social transmission, mastered a sequence that lay beyond individual solution under the experimental conditions.

## 8. Funeral architecture

In elephants, returns to remains, touching, investigation, attempts to lift the dead, guarding of the body, circling around it, prolonged immobility, and changes in social interactions have been documented. In these contexts trumpet calls, rumbles, chirps, and roars have been recorded.

In 2024 the authors of a field report described five found bodies of Asian elephant calves as cases of burial. The bodies lay in irrigation ditches in a characteristic posture; head, trunk, and dorsal region were covered with soil, while the legs remained directed upward. Compacted earth and tracks of several elephants supported a reconstruction of joint displacement and covering. In some cases information about transfer and vocalisation came from workers on tea plantations.

These data are assembled as five situationally discovered field cases. They join the state of the bodies, the arrangement of the place, tracks, and witness reports. Such a form supports reconstruction of concrete episodes; assessment of the frequency of the behaviour, of its prevalence, and of its intentionality requires systematic observations.

In cetaceans, especially dolphins and orcas, prolonged carrying of dead calves, holding the body at the surface, accompaniment by other group members, and extended presence nearby have been recorded. Acoustic activity has been recorded during the carrying of dead calves — two encounters, reported by their authors as preliminary observations. During one documented net-entanglement event that ended in the death of a group member, a pronounced peak of individual whistles and pulsed signals was measured in a group of bottlenose dolphins.

By funeral architecture is meant here an operational configuration: the dead body becomes a focus, and the living reorganise relative to it contact, distance, movement, vocalisation, group coordination, space, and the duration of behaviour.

In elephants this configuration includes touching remains and, in the five described cases, a reconstructed transformation of space through burial. In cetaceans water translates the bond into supporting, carrying, and accompanying the body. In both cases the sequence is preserved in time after the death of a conspecific.

In human phenomenological language such vocal and motor sequences may be heard as a farewell song and seen as a dance around the dead. The ethological layer fixes their measurable form: type of signal, movement, duration, contact, and coordination.

Vocal and motor accompaniment of death has been measured. Elephant burials are presented as documented reconstructions of five episodes. Internal semantics form a field of admissible interpretations that can be narrowed by the comparison of heterogeneous facets.

The encoding is obliged to separate one-step extensions: if histories $\gamma$ and $\gamma'$ differ in their last index or in the value at it — in particular, sister branches with a common last index and distinct last states — their codes differ. Otherwise the check of coherence between the selected Unit and the code of the shell branch, declared in §5, is empty — both sister branches would answer to one code, and the requirement would exclude no assignment whatever. This condition is an axiom of the declared encoding, of the same register as the injectivity class of $\nu$: at the admission of §15 the separation conjuncts check the realised pairs of codes, while the separation of arbitrary distinct histories remains an obligation of the declaration.

From this follows a condition on the cardinality of the field forming the I. The Unit of a line is constant along an identity-preserving continuation while the code of its history changes at every step, so the witness of operator correctness of §15 must differ from step to step and lie in one layer $\mathfrak F_{\mathrm I,i}^{[\ell,R]}$. The cardinality of the layer is obliged to the declared model rather than fixed in advance: for every $N$, a line preserving its identity over $N$ steps requires no fewer than $N+1$ distinct field representatives, so a model admitting identity-preserving lines of every length is obliged to layers of unbounded cardinality — $\sup_i\bigl|\mathfrak F_{\mathrm I,i}^{[\ell,R]}\bigr|=\infty$, a single layer being obliged to be infinite only where a single line preserves its identity over steps without bound — while for a model whose declared criterion bounds the length of its lines the bound owed is read from its reachable admission structure, not from line length alone: at every reachable pair of a line and a moment the universal witness conjunct of $\operatorname{WF}_{W,R}$ in §15 obliges pairwise distinct representatives for all codes of the line's active field — $q_{\mathrm{sys}}^{[R]}$ is a function, so distinct codes force distinct witnesses — lying in fibres disjoint across simultaneously active Units, and the layer is obliged to dominate the total of these obligations; the per-line succession of $N+1$ witnesses is the sub-obligation contributed by length alone — the acyclic model of the second example of §15 admits no history of more than two states, and its total obligation remains finite while exceeding that per-line count. Where the obligation is unbounded, it concerns the stock of witnesses as a whole — the representatives must lie in $\operatorname{Dom}(\iota_\ell^{[R]})\cap\operatorname{Dom}(q_{\mathrm{sys}}^{[R]})$, and a finite domain of either projection would cut a long identity-preserving line short in the same way a finite layer would. The finiteness spoken of by the realisation conditions of §15 concerns the shell encoding and the output fields, not $\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}$; the operator register is nowhere declared finite.

## 10. Nested substrates

Nesting turns any action into a multilayered event. At the same time, structural inclusion and autonomous nesting are different types of relation.

The relation $\preceq$ in this work denotes broad structural inclusion: a state of the lower enters into the composition of the upper. The direct strict case of ONTOΣ XIV further requires a declared embedding morphism

$$
\nu:S_i\longrightarrow S
$$

and an intrinsic budget of each substrate. Let $\mathcal H_X$ be the space of histories of $X$, and $\operatorname{Adm}_R(X)\subseteq\mathcal H_X$ the declared class of admissible histories. The notation $h\sqsubseteq h'$ means that $h$ is a prefix of $h'$, and $\operatorname{len}_E(h)$ denotes the number of edges traversed. A history throughout is an index-function — a map from an initial segment of its index domain to states — an edge is a pair of consecutive indices, $\operatorname{len}_E$ counts those pairs, and the concatenation $h\frown e$ names the one-edge extension assigned to the event by the declared correspondence below, so the index-function and edge readings are one presentation, not two. The declared class is closed under prefixes, shift and concatenation decomposition; prefix closure is necessary, since otherwise the immediate predecessor of the first crossing edge could fail to belong to the class, its load would then be undefined, and an actually completed crossing would not enter $\operatorname{FP}_X$. The remaining two closures are read by no condition of the present work: they travel with the imported signature, and neither carries an invariance of the load — the load is declared on histories, while the additive scheme below prices an extension rather than a state edge, so a shifted or decomposed segment is a fresh argument of the load and inherits no value from the position it was cut from. The cumulative irreversible load is typed on admissible histories:

$$
\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}:
\operatorname{Adm}_R(X)
\longrightarrow
\mathbb R_{\ge0}\sqcup\{\bot\},
\qquad
h\sqsubseteq h'
\Longrightarrow
\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h)
\le
\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h'),
\qquad
\operatorname{len}_E(h)=0
\Longrightarrow
\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h)=0.
$$

The zero-length condition fixes the base of the accumulation. The value $\bot$ arises exactly on histories containing an edge with an unrecorded contribution of the coordinate $\mathrm{mon}$; the monotonicity inequality and all comparisons with capacity are read under definedness of both sides, and verdicts over such a history inherit the value unknown. The map $u_X:\mathcal H_X\to U_X$ is retained as the reading of the attained intrinsic-clock index, where $U_X$ is a discretely linearly ordered set of indices with relation $\le_X$; $U_X$ is not the domain of the load. No condition of the present work reads $u_X$: the map travels with the imported signature so that the load layer keeps the typing of its source, where the intrinsic clock keeps its role. The capacity $C_X>0$ is pre-declared and finite: an infinite capacity trivialises budgetary discrimination, so the narrowing of the codomain to $\mathbb R_{\ge0}$ is intentional. The morphism $\nu$ is injective on states, carries $\mathcal E_{+}$-valued admissible histories to admissible ones and commutes with prefix restriction, that is, $\nu_*$ preserves the relation $\sqsubseteq$; on a closing history, whose final vertex is the cessation marker, $\nu_*$ is not defined and is not read. The notation $\nu\colon S_i\longrightarrow S$ is read as a model-declared function of states $\nu\colon\operatorname{St}(S_i)\to\operatorname{St}(S)$ on the declared state spaces of the carriers $\operatorname{St}(\cdot)\subseteq\mathcal E_{+}$, acting on histories pointwise, $\nu_*h:=\nu\circ h$. The morphism $\nu$ is not declared budget-respecting: such a requirement would reduce the Independent-Exhaustion Test to a comparison of capacities. The residual budget is defined by:

$$
b_X(h)
:=
C_X-
\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h),
\qquad
h\in\operatorname{Adm}_R(X),\;\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h)\neq\bot.
$$

For a history $h\in\operatorname{Adm}_R(X)$ with defined load, $\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h)\neq\bot$, we introduce the set of already attained capacity crossings — the load of every prefix of such a history is then defined, and the capacity comparison inside is read totally:

$$
\operatorname{Cross}_X(h)
:=
\bigl\{
h'\in\operatorname{Adm}_R(X)
\bigm|
h'\sqsubseteq h
\;\land\;
\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h')\ge C_X
\bigr\}.
$$

The domain of first-passage-admissible histories is defined by:

$$
\operatorname{FP}_X
:=
\Bigl\{
h\in\operatorname{Adm}_R(X),\;\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h)\neq\bot
\Bigm|
\exists h^\star\in\operatorname{Cross}_X(h)\;
\Bigl(
\bigl(
\forall h'\in\operatorname{Cross}_X(h):
h^\star\sqsubseteq h'
\bigr)
\;\land\;
\exists h^{-}\,\exists e:\;
h^\star=h^{-}\frown e
\;\land\;
\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h^{-})<C_X
\Bigr)
\Bigr\}.
$$

For $h\in\operatorname{FP}_X$ the prefixes of a fixed history are linearly ordered by $\sqsubseteq$, so such a least prefix is unique; we denote it $h_X^\star(h)$. The least crossing prefix crosses at its own last edge by the displayed conjunct of $\operatorname{FP}_X$: $h_X^\star(h)=h^{-}\frown e$ with $\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h^{-})<C_X$ — a history all of whose finite prefixes stay below capacity while a limit member attains it has no crossing edge, is realised by no update of §15, and does not enter $\operatorname{FP}_X$: first passage is an executable exhaustion, not a limit. Over the whole class $\operatorname{Adm}_R(X)$ the prefix order forms a tree rather than a chain, and global linearity is not claimed.

The test is read only at a declared nested pair: $S_i\neq S$, the pair $S_i\preceq S$ is a declared nesting link carrying the strict status of XIV, and $\nu$ is the embedding declared for that link — a triple outside this domain does not enter the test, so a carrier cannot present itself, through an injective relabelling of its own states, as its own lower substrate. The triple $(S,S_i,\nu)$ is autonomously nested in the sense of the Independent-Exhaustion Test when an admissible lower history and its admissible image in the upper substrate yield:

$$
\operatorname{IE}_R(S,S_i,\nu)=1
\iff
\exists h\in
\operatorname{Adm}_R(S_i)
\cap
\operatorname{FP}_{S_i}:
\Bigl(
h
=
h_{S_i}^{\star}(h)
\;\land\;
\operatorname{len}_E(h)\ge1
\;\land\;
h\ \text{is valued in }\mathcal E_{+}\ \text{throughout}
\;\land\;
\Phi_{S}^{\mathrm{cum}}
\bigl(
\nu_*h
\bigr)
\neq\bot
\;\land\;
h\ \text{is an initial segment of the realised history of }S_i,
\ \text{counted from the initial index}
\;\land\;
\nu_*h\ \text{coincides, after re-indexing by the birth offset, with the}
\ \text{restriction of the realised history of }S
\ \text{to the ambient interval that }h\ \text{occupies}
\;\land\;
\Phi_{S}^{\mathrm{cum}}
\bigl(
H_S^{\upharpoonright h}
\bigr)
\neq\bot
\;\land\;
\Phi_{S}^{\mathrm{cum}}
\bigl(
H_S^{\upharpoonright h}
\bigr)
<
C_S
\Bigr).
$$

The conditions $h\in\operatorname{FP}_{S_i}$ and $h=h_{S_i}^{\star}(h)$ already fix the history exactly up to the first prefix at which $\Phi_{S_i}^{\mathrm{cum}}\ge C_{S_i}$; therefore the lower threshold is not duplicated in the formula for $\operatorname{IE}_R$. The non-strict inequality $\ge$ inherited from the definition of first crossing preserves the discrete jump across capacity. The condition $\operatorname{len}_E(h)\ge1$ is written for readability and is logically redundant: the zero-length history carries zero load by the base of the accumulation and, under positive capacity, does not enter the crossing set, so the exclusion of an initial condition follows from the remaining conjuncts rather than being added by this one. Admissibility of $\nu_*h$ is not required separately: it follows from the declared type of $\nu$. Definedness of the upper load enters the witness condition: a candidate with an unrecorded load does not certify the pair. The upper threshold is read on the upper's own realised prefix: $H_S^{\upharpoonright h}$ denotes the realised history of $S$ truncated at the right endpoint of the ambient interval the witness occupies — accumulation from the upper's own base, the full cost of everything the upper has run up to that moment, so the conjunct compares the upper's residual budget against its capacity; the transported image, whose re-indexed accumulation starts at the zero base and measures the witness interval alone, is read by the valuedness and transport conjuncts and by no threshold — an upper carrier already exhausted before the witness interval thereby fails the test, as the domain declaration of this test demands. The threshold stands last in the display and under its own definedness guard: read after the realisation conjuncts, the truncation names an existing interval of the upper's realised history — a candidate the lower never realised is refused by the realisation conjunct before the truncation is read — and at an unrecorded earlier edge of the upper's prefix the guard $\Phi_{S}^{\mathrm{cum}}(H_S^{\upharpoonright h})\neq\bot$ fails, $\operatorname{IE}_R$ returns $0$, and the codomain stays Boolean — the comparison is never read at $\bot$. Both loads are computed on the intrinsic admissible histories of the substrates and are read on one realisation of the nested pair, on both of its sides: the witness $h$ is an initial segment of the lower carrier's own realised history, counted from its initial index — the crossing is a property of the prefix, since the cumulative ledger accumulates from the base — while its transported image $\nu_*h$ is the restriction of the upper carrier's realised history to the ambient interval that $h$ occupies — and that interval is determinate, not a choice: the realised history of each carrier occupies the per-carrier interval domain of the state map $\operatorname{state}_R$ declared in §11, the left endpoint of the lower carrier's interval is its birth moment, and the ambient interval named is the image of $\operatorname{Dom}(h)$ under the offset by that endpoint — the offset sends the history index $n$ to the $n$-fold successor of that endpoint in $\mathbb T_R$, a term of the declared successor structure requiring no further map between the registers, since every observed prefix is finite — so the reading is fixed by declared data for a lower substrate born at any moment; the equality of the conjunct is read after re-indexing by that same offset — once the shift is applied the transported image and the restriction are functions on one index set, not two functions on two domains — the two realisation conjuncts of the display above, so that a triple violating either does not certify whatever the other conjuncts return. The lower conjunct carries its own load: without it an admissible crossing path the lower carrier never realised — the realised history running elsewhere while a counterfactual prefix exhausts the ledger on paper — would certify an exhaustion no execution realised; conjugation along $\nu$ reads the two sides synchronously over the witness interval — a lower substrate born later than its upper carrier is thereby not excluded by the upper's earlier birth — and without the upper conjunct the test would compare a lower exhaustion against an upper history admissible only in isolation. The realisation read here is the declared one: the realised histories of $S$ and $S_i$ are the trajectories the state map $\operatorname{state}_R$, typed in §11, keeps for the carriers — each realisation conjunct reads a declared coordinate of the model, not an unnamed realisation, and a model exhibiting an IE-triple carries that map with the triple. The valuedness conjunct carries the transportability of the witness: a first passage realised on the closing edge of a ceasing carrier exhausts its ledger but is not $\nu$-transportable — its final edge ends at the cessation vertex, which has no image among the upper states — and certifies no IE pair; the crossing set and the ledger keep such a history, the test does not read it, and every IE-witness thereby carries $\operatorname{len}_E(\nu_*h)=\operatorname{len}_E(h)\ge1$ literally, with no prefix reading involved. The conjunction of the display is read guardedly — by the same convention under which the implication lists of the transition record and the state comparisons of §11 are read: each conjunct is read only when the preceding ones hold, so the transport terms and the realisation conjuncts are read only under the valuedness conjunct; at a closing history that conjunct fails, the later terms are not read, and $\operatorname{IE}_R$ returns $0$ rather than lacking a value — the predicate is total on its declared domain, by evaluation order and not by a partial term read strictly. The lower budget is exhausted, the upper remains positive. If the lower has no intrinsic load, capacity or independent exhaustion, it remains a structural part or a role, but does not receive the strict status of an autonomous nested substrate of XIV. Configurations in which the upper is exhausted while the lower is retained, or in which both are exhausted, break the nesting from above and do not fall within the domain of this test.

ONTOΣ XV supplies a prior criterion for the upper carrier. In a finite formal class:

$$
\operatorname{Nest}_{\mathrm{IE}}^{\mathrm{formal}}(S)=1
\iff
\exists\gamma\in\operatorname{Adm}_R(S):
\operatorname{len}_{E}(\gamma)\ge1,
\qquad
\Phi_S^{\mathrm{cum}}
\bigl(\gamma\bigr)\neq\bot,
\qquad
\Phi_S^{\mathrm{cum}}
\bigl(\gamma\bigr)<C_S.
$$

Here $\operatorname{Adm}_R(S)\subseteq\mathcal H_S$, and $\operatorname{len}_{E}$ is introduced above in this section. Nestability is defined by the existence of an admissible trajectory that traverses at least one edge and remains below capacity, not by geometric volume or number of states — and not by the state of the realised accumulation: under prefix closure and the prefix monotonicity of the load the existential is decided by the affordability of a single admissible edge — the one-edge prefix of any witness is admissible, carries a defined load, and carries no more of it than the witness — and the criterion is deliberately this weak — the outer class of the ladder certifies capability of budgeted motion, while the realised ledger is read by the crossing set, the first-passage test, and the Independent-Exhaustion witness, not by this prior criterion. A one-point trajectory of zero length is excluded. In the imported formal criterion XV.1 a degenerate edge with zero increment of the budgetary coordinate $\mathrm{mon}$ remains a boundary case until identification with an executable register. In the present work it does not mean zero full cost: if motion is realised as an event, $S\in\operatorname{Part}_R(e)$ — the set of participants is defined below in this section — and the coordinate $\mathrm{mon}$ is declared, then $\Phi_{S,\mathrm{mon}}(e;\Delta t)>0$. This criterion certifies formal IE-nestability. Physical autonomy requires a pre-specified lower dynamics, an intrinsic budget and a carrier of a witness.

Let $\mathsf{Nest}_{\mathrm{formal}}$ be the class of carriers $S$ for which $\operatorname{Nest}_{\mathrm{IE}}^{\mathrm{formal}}(S)=1$; $\mathsf{Nest}_{\mathrm{aut}}$ its subclass of carriers for which the model exhibits a triple $(S,S_i,\nu)$ with $\operatorname{IE}_R(S,S_i,\nu)=1$, where in addition the declaration of the lower substrate is sealed before the event, the quantities $\Phi_{S_i}^{\mathrm{cum}}$ and $C_{S_i}$ are not derived from the witnessing history, and the lower dynamics carries a non-trivial realised cycle — the step relation read from the declared class of admissible histories of the lower substrate, $x\to_{S_i}y$ exactly when some $h\in\operatorname{Adm}_R(S_i)$ carries adjacent indices with $h(t)=x$ and $h(t+1)=y$, contains a closed path $x_0\to\dots\to x_m=x_0$ of length $m\ge2$ with pairwise distinct $x_0,\dots,x_{m-1}$, so that a reflexive loop does not count as a cycle, and the path is obliged to be traversed by the realised history of the lower substrate — consecutive indices of the trajectory the state map $\operatorname{state}_R$ of §11 keeps for the carrier, running through $x_0,\dots,x_m$: a path assembled from edges of mutually incompatible histories is a fact about the derived graph and not a realised return of the dynamics, and the class is written for the latter — a return, not a claim of repetition: what the clause demands is one admissible history closing the cycle, and the word is chosen to match exactly that — the relation is thereby a function of data the model already declares, and no separate dynamics object enters the class; $\mathsf{Nest}_{\mathrm{wit}}$ the subclass of the latter in which a carrier of a topological witness is declared. Non-triviality of motion under transfer is not an additional hypothesis here: $\nu_*h=\nu\circ h$ preserves the index domain, $\nu$ is injective on states and, by its declared transport property, carries $\mathcal E_{+}$-valued admissible histories to admissible ones — the IE-witness is such a history by its valuedness conjunct — so it supplies $\nu_*h\in\operatorname{Adm}_R(S)$ with $\operatorname{len}_E(\nu_*h)=\operatorname{len}_E(h)\ge1$, while the witness of formal nestability is the upper carrier's own truncated prefix $H_S^{\upharpoonright h}$, on which the conjuncts of the test supply every requirement of the criterion at once — the budget ones its definedness and its threshold, the realisation ones the interval it occupies: a prefix of the realised history of $S$, hence admissible by prefix closure, with $\Phi_S^{\mathrm{cum}}(H_S^{\upharpoonright h})\neq\bot$, $\Phi_S^{\mathrm{cum}}(H_S^{\upharpoonright h})<C_S$, and at least $\operatorname{len}_E(h)\ge1$ edges, its right endpoint being the $\operatorname{len}_E(h)$-fold successor of the lower carrier's birth moment while its left endpoint is the upper carrier's own base. The transported image carries no threshold here — its load is read by the valuedness conjunct for definedness and by nothing else — and the omission is not an economy: the accumulation prices an extension rather than a state edge — one state edge lawfully carries two prices under two events, as the event–window correspondence declared below in this section states — so $\nu_*h$, a segment re-based at the zero index rather than a prefix of $H_S^{\upharpoonright h}$ wherever the lower carrier is born later than the upper, inherits no bound from it, and the closure of the class under shift carries no invariance of the load under that shift. The inclusions below therefore hold at a fixed declared model without a proviso:

$$
\mathsf{Nest}_{\mathrm{formal}}
\supseteq
\mathsf{Nest}_{\mathrm{aut}}
\supseteq
\mathsf{Nest}_{\mathrm{wit}}.
$$

Both inclusions are read within a fixed declared model, and in that reading both are non-strict. The separation of the first pair is an existence claim, not a property of every model: the imported construction exhibits a model with a carrier that is formally nestable yet admits no IE-certified pair under the conditions listed, and there it is precisely the requirement of a non-trivial realised cycle that separates one class from the other. In a particular declared model the difference of the classes may turn out to be empty, and the first inclusion then holds as an equality; the work claims nothing to the contrary. Strictness of the second inclusion is not asserted at any level: the source does not claim it, and the present work adds no proof of its own.

Therefore the axiom "everything is structure" opens the possibility of systemic distinction of each node, but does not replace verification of its autonomy.

For direct IE-certified pairs, XIV.1 isolates a class-level existential asymmetry. In the present work it is introduced as a separate testable hypothesis and is not derived from a single IE. Let $\mathcal K_R^{\mathrm{IE}}$ be the declared class of quintuples $(S,S_i,\nu,e,\Delta t)$ in which the pair is IE-certified, both carriers participate in the event, that is $S,S_i\in\operatorname{Part}_R(e)$ with the coordinate $\mathrm{mon}$ declared for both, and the budgetary coordinates of the event are comparable in one window under a declared additive budgetary scheme; the capacities of both carriers are the per-carrier constants declared with the model — values of the declared measurement procedure, fixed by the declaration, and therefore not varying across the witnesses of one declaration. The participation requirement is not decorative: without it the numerator $\Phi_{S,\mathrm{mon}}(e;\Delta t)$ may vanish, and then a single quintuple satisfies the condition for all $\varepsilon$ at once, contrary to the need for a sequence of witnesses claimed below. We call this class asymptotically asymmetric if:

$$
\exists c\in(0,1]\;
\exists S\;
\exists\kappa>0\;
\forall\varepsilon>0\;
\exists(S_i,\nu,e,\Delta t):
\;
(S,S_i,\nu,e,\Delta t)
\in\mathcal K_R^{\mathrm{IE}},
\quad
C_{S_i}\ge\kappa,
\quad
\frac{\Phi_{S,\mathrm{mon}}(e;\Delta t)}{C_S}<\varepsilon,
\qquad
\frac{\Phi_{S_i,\mathrm{mon}}(e;\Delta t)}{C_{S_i}}\ge c.
$$

One and the same constant $c$ works for all $\varepsilon$, and one and the same upper carrier is bound before $\varepsilon$ deliberately: an inner existential rebinding both carriers per $\varepsilon$ would be satisfiable by inflating capacities across a family of unrelated wholes — the upper ratios driven to zero with no pair exhibiting any asymmetry — and the fixity of each carrier's declared capacity could not block that, since it fixes the capacity of each carrier, not the family of carriers ranged over. The lower side is bound symmetrically: the capacities of the lower carriers of the witnesses are bounded below by the declared $\kappa$ — without it the mirrored manoeuvre, deflating $C_{S_i}$ along the sequence, would satisfy the display with the two absolute consumptions of every witnessing pair equal and no pair asymmetric; under both bindings the witnesses' upper consumptions vanish absolutely while their lower consumptions stay at or above $c\,\kappa$. The formula asserts the existence of such events in the declared class, not their presence for every pair and every event. Its satisfaction requires an asymptotic, parametric or generative class with a sequence of witnesses; a finite empirical sample can check only a pre-declared finite separation threshold and does not certify the universal quantifier $\forall\varepsilon>0$. The capacity discipline is carried by the declaration rather than by a caveat: a redeclared $C_S$ is a different model, whose quintuples witness nothing for this one, and within one declaration every witness reads the fixed capacity of its carrier.

The present work also considers the cross-branch case. Let $S^{*}$ be a declared common whole, and $H\preceq^{*}S^{*}$ and $S_i\preceq^{*}S^{*}$ the initiating human carrier and the affected swarm; the reflexive-transitive closure $\preceq^{*}$ is defined in §11. The relation $H\preceq^{*}S_i$ is not required for this example: hand and swarm occupy neighbouring branches within a common whole. Therefore the comparison of their registers below is a hypothesis of a composite model $R$ resting on the architecture of XIV, not a direct substitution into XIV.1.

Motion of the hand carries a positive monotone consumption for $H$ and is at the same time able to close the former identity of $S_i$. For the human this is a gesture. For the swarm in a boundary-layer flow of motion it is a vortex that reorganises the medium, the trajectories and the admissible continuation.

Without this requirement the model may leave the destroyed substrate outside the declared scope and build no register for it at all, reproducing in the formalism precisely the omission that §13 calls the error of isolation.

Under a declared additive budgetary scheme accumulation proceeds along the edges of an admissible history. The model must declare the correspondence between the event window and history extension: realisation of event $e$ in the window $\Delta t$ extends the history $h$ of carrier $X$ by exactly one edge, and the resulting admissible history is denoted $h\frown e$. The correspondence is read at carriers with a realised pre-event history: the event that brings a carrier into being does not extend a history of the newborn but initialises it at the one-point base, so the creation cost demanded by participation is read by the event register and by the edges of the pre-existing participants of the creating event, while the cumulative load of the created carrier starts at the base — the ledger of a line prices its continuations, not its birth, and charging the newborn's own budget with its birth would spend, before the first continuation, capacity that no continuation of that carrier has consumed; cessation is not symmetric to birth in this respect: a ceasing carrier does have a pre-event state, so the closing event extends the history of a ceasing carrier by its final edge, on which every accumulating coordinate of that event — a terminal act of holding included — is booked before cessation, and only after that edge does the ledger receive no further one; what birth lacks is the pre-event state, not the cost. The final edge is typed by the same cessation marker as the closure record of §11: the history space of the carrier admits $\partial_{\mathrm{car}}$ as a terminal vertex only — the closing extension $h\frown e$ ends at $\partial_{\mathrm{car}}$, the vertex carries no outgoing edge, and $\operatorname{Adm}_R(X)$ is closed under exactly this extension at the closing event, so $\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}(h\frown e)$ reads a typed argument; every reader of states — the criterion, the encodings, the state comparisons — reads the $\mathcal E_{+}$-prefix of such a history, from which the closing history differs by the final edge alone, while the transport $\nu_*$ reads no closing history at all — the IE witness is scoped by its valuedness conjunct to histories valued in $\mathcal E_{+}$ throughout: the marker enters accumulation and nothing else. The correspondence is injective on extensions: to each admissible extension $h\frown e$ the declaration assigns its unique realising event and window — one extension carrying two prices, whether from one event under two windows or from two events realising one state edge, would demand two values of $\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}$ on one argument, and a model admitting such an extension lies outside the declared class: the cumulative load thereby remains a single-valued function on $\operatorname{Adm}_R(X)$, and the accumulation equality of §16 reads the same unique assignment through its window-indexed embeddings. Without this correspondence the event-and-window quantity $\Phi_{X,\mathrm{mon}}(e;\Delta t)$ and the history-indexed $\Phi_X^{\mathrm{cum}}$ remain incommensurable, and the additive scheme unwritable. The homology class of the declared cycle enters the declaration of the model on a par with $V_R$ and $c_R$: on a cycle bounding a declared probe surface the readout equals the flux of $d\omega$ through it and reads the local differential channel, while on a non-null-homologous cycle it also picks up the component that pairs with the topological class; the present work chooses neither regime for the model and reads no verdict about the witness from $\sigma_{S_i}^{(M)}$. The bridge is written in the separated regime $\alpha_{S_i}=1$ and is subject to the admissibility bound $c_R\left|\sigma_{S_i}^{(M)}(\nu,e;\Delta t)\right|\le b_{S_i}(h)-\Phi_{S_i}^{(\mathrm X)}(e;\Delta t)$, where $h$ is the admissible history the event extends, with positive residue, $b_{S_i}(h)>0$; on the edge realising the first crossing of capacity the bound is not read — read there, it would confine every first crossing to exact attainment of capacity, the bound on an edge being equivalent in the separated regime to $\Phi_{S_i}^{\mathrm{cum}}(h\frown e)\le C_{S_i}$, and the witness class of the independent-exhaustion test would degenerate to that knife-edge: this is a condition on the declaration and not a theorem, since the constant and the measured circulation are independent inputs and are able to violate it. The right-hand side is written through the residual budget rather than the full capacity: the decomposition here is carried in the register of an event increment, whereas in the source it is written for the cumulative pair, and comparing an increment against the full capacity would not bound the accumulation at all — with a nearly exhausted budget it would admit a declaration carrying the substrate past $C_{S_i}$ on that very step. ## 11. Metamorphosis of the lower and the retained field of the upper

Here metamorphosis of the lower covers both typed cases of §5: transformation of form with preservation of the line, and metamorphosis of identity. Let $S\in\operatorname{Anc}^{*}(S_i)$; the set of ancestors is defined later in this section. Before the event this whole contains $S_i$ with identity $\mathcal I_i^{[\ell]}$. After the event the line $i$ receives a realised outcome: the state of the carrier may change, and the identity may be preserved, replaced, branched, or may close the former trajectory. Change of state below receives a separate predicate; branching, change of identity, and change of the retained field remain independent grounds for change of geometry.

The future of the whole $S$ is already constructed from the new composition.

Let $\mathbf{Sys}_R$ be the set of permanent carriers of the model — a set and not a proper class: the ancestor collections of this section and the closures of §16 are typed as sets, and a class-sized collection of carriers would leave them without a value — and $\mathbb T_R$ a discretely linearly ordered set of temporal indices with a least element and a total successor operation, every index reached from the least element by finitely many successor steps: the notations $t+1$, $s\le t$ and $\bigcup_{s\le t}J_s$ are read in that order, and without it they would carry no meaning, while a successor component not reached from the least element would anchor the registry recurrences of §15 to nothing — the non-reuse invariants are carried by induction from the declared initial state, and induction reaches exactly the successor orbit of the least element. The state map is typed as partial, and its domain tracks the existence of the carrier: a total map would assign a shell state to a carrier before its initialisation and after the closure of its line, whereas $\mathcal E_{+}$ holds no element for absence. The tracking is single-episode by declaration: for each carrier the defined slice $\{t\mid(X,t)\in\operatorname{Dom}(\operatorname{state}_R)\}$ is an interval of $\mathbb T_R$ — a ceased carrier does not return, the carrier-level mirror of the non-return of closed lines — so the realised history of a carrier is one object, not a scatter of episodes: the admissible history assembled from the carrier's one-point base by the event–window correspondence of §10 along the realised trajectory, whose $\mathcal E_{+}$-vertex track is precisely this slice — at a closing event the assembly ends with the single terminal vertex $\partial_{\mathrm{car}}$ beyond the slice, per the final-edge typing of §10 — and it is this object that the budget layer reads in full, the closing edge booked, while the realisation conjuncts of the Independent-Exhaustion Test read its $\mathcal E_{+}$-prefix, their witness being $\mathcal E_{+}$-valued throughout; a slice with a gap would leave those conjuncts without truth conditions, since the trajectory the map keeps would then be no history at all. All comparisons of states below are read under definedness of both sides. The map keeps the realised history of carriers: the families of states inside the transition record of §15 are the record's own data of its event, and the coherence condition of the realised transition, declared there, ties them to the map.

## 13. Isolation as method

Modern knowledge has learned to isolate an object, fix variables, and measure a local effect. This method has given science enormous precision.

The problem arises at the moment when methodological isolation is turned into a picture of the world itself.

A variable temporarily bracketed out of the experiment continues to exist in the common structure. An action that is small in a chosen upper-level coordinate is capable of changing nested states and identities only when the corresponding witnesses are present. The inner sense of an animal, inaccessible to a single instrument, leaves traces on other measurable facets.

We have taken the convenience of separate computation for a model of separate existence.

Humanity has accumulated an enormous volume of knowledge about shells. Zero architecture shows the scale of the next task: to bind them through $\mathbf 0_W$, the fields of formation of the I, Unit-identities, typed nested costs, and admissibility fields.

The event $e$ unfolds in a reference order of four obligatory operations and one conditional one; the contract of $\mathcal N_R$ is extensional and checks the returned tuple, not the sequence of calls: a realisation that obtains the same tuple in another order satisfies the contract, and operations whose results do not enter the tuple are not observable by the contract:

1. update of composition $U_e$: from $(S_t,\{S_{i,t}\}_{i\in J_t},J_t)$ and the event it produces the resulting upper state $S_{t+1}$; the realised resulting states of the lines with a predecessor are not its output — they are read per line from the parent-relative $\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{real},\ell}^{e[R]}$ of operation 2, a term that reads the identities, and the states of lines without a predecessor are read from the realised initiations of the event — the finite declared family of fresh-carrier initial states the event brings into being, consumed by the initialisation of operation 4 — while the indexed presentation $\{S_{j,t+1}\}_{j\in J_{t+1}}$, with its index set $J_{t+1}$, arises only in the index-hygiene step of $U_{12}^{[R,\ell]}$, which reads the identities and the standing registries that decide retention, metamorphosis, and freshness — the composition update cannot see them, and an assignment of descendant states or of indices typed on the shell composition alone would demand two values of one function on states differing only in their identity coordinates;
2. for each $i\in J_t$, computation of the parent-relative $\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{real},\ell}^{e[R]}(\mathcal I_{i,t}^{[\ell]},S_{i,t})$, then construction of $p_t$, $D_t$ and $B_t$; the generator-relative $\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{adm},\ell}^{[R,\mathrm c]}(S_{i,t})$ serves the openness readings of §6 and does not enter the transition contract at all: neither it, nor the generator $\operatorname{Cand}_\ell^{[R]}$, nor the crown predicate is read by the domain $\operatorname{Dom}(\mathcal N_R)$, enters the record $\mathsf U_{t+1}^{[R,\ell]}$, or is returned in the tuple, so two models differing only in their generator are indistinguishable to the transition contract, and this caveat has the status of a statement: the crown verdict does not factor through the transition record — such a pair of models gives one record different crown verdicts — and the admission validators do not certify the crown; openness is a separate analytical operation on the same state, and its decidability is required for the readings of §6, not for admission — while the unrestricted $\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{adm},\ell}^{[R]}$ remains extensional and is not subject to computation;

With the map of §11 the record is tied by the coherence condition of the realised transition: for the transition realised by the top-level history, $\operatorname{state}_R(\operatorname{car}_R(j),t+1)=S_{j,t+1}$, and the state-change predicate reads the map — the realised world; on inputs not realised by the history the map assigns no values of level $t+1$. The composition components of the record are likewise not free, and each carries its own producer: the upper state $S_{t+1}$ is the output of the declared composition update $U_e\bigl(S_t,\{S_{i,t}\}_{i\in J_t},J_t\bigr)$; the provenance-keyed states of the lines with a predecessor are pinned by the bijection of $\operatorname{RealHistStep}_{R,\ell}^{e}$ with $\operatorname{Succ}_{\mathrm{real},\ell}^{e[R]}$ — a term that reads the identities, which the shell-typed update does not: a shell-typed producer of these states would demand two values of one function on inputs differing only in their identity coordinates, by the same argument that removes the index assignment from the update; the states of lines without a predecessor are pinned by the realised initiations of the event under the one-point rule $\operatorname{InitHist}_{R,\ell}$; and the index set $J_{t+1}$ with its assignment over the descendant family is produced by the index-hygiene step from the identities and the registries of the input; an input whose record — the value $U_{12}^{[R,\ell]}(\mathrm{in}_t)$ — has an upper state that differs from the update's output, whose descendant states differ from these pins, or whose index assignment differs from the hygiene step's, does not belong to $\operatorname{Dom}(U_{12}^{[R,\ell]})$: the pins are conditions on the input through the record the declared map assigns to it, not properties of a free-standing record. The same criterion reads the coupling of state and carrier: an input whose record carries two indices with one carrier bearing distinct states on either boundary — $\operatorname{car}_R(j)=\operatorname{car}_R(k)\Rightarrow S_{j,t+1}=S_{k,t+1}$ for $j,k\in J_{t+1}$ and $\operatorname{car}_R(i)=\operatorname{car}_R(i')\Rightarrow S_{i,t}=S_{i',t}$ for $i,i'\in J_t$ — does not belong to $\operatorname{Dom}(U_{12}^{[R,\ell]})$; the condition is computable from the extended map and the states of the record itself. Partiality is thereby not a freedom of the implementation: membership in the domain is fixed by a criterion — an input belongs to $\operatorname{Dom}(U_{12}^{[R,\ell]})$ exactly when the joint update of composition, the map of origin, and the assignment of identities exist in the declared model and coincide with the declared producers named above; an empty domain under a non-empty declared class of events with realised continuers is a trivialisation of the computational nucleus, parallel to the identically unit $K_\ell$, and does not belong to the declared class. The criterion reads the effect, not a single route to it: a declared admission predicate identically false on the reachable contexts of a non-empty structural core empties every extensional field and, through the own-field membership conjuncts, the domain with them — silence through empty fields is not distinguished from silence through empty procedure domains, and a model reaching the empty domain by either route lies outside the declared class.

The same conjunct stands at the input boundary, for every $i\in J_t$: a state whose realised history is rejected by its own admissibility predicate is not an input — otherwise the closure of admission would hold over the transition and break at its beginning. The input boundary is closed along five further coordinates: the active indices must belong to the union of what was issued, $J_t\subseteq\bigcup_{s\le t}J_s$ — an active index never issued declares its prehistory falsely in the same way a closed index never issued would — the addresses of active lines must belong to the registry of used ones, the active indices must be disjoint from the registry of closed ones (a closed line does not return active: this is not derivable from sets of what was issued, and the registry $\mathsf{Cls}$ exists for precisely that reason), the registry of closed ones must lie in the union of what was issued, $\mathsf{Cls}_{\le t}^{[\ell]}\subseteq\bigcup_{s\le t}J_s$ — a closed index never issued declares its prehistory falsely, and freshness outside the union of the issued thereby entails freshness outside the closed, — and the terminal value of the observed prefix of each history must coincide with the state of its line. The completeness of both procedures enters the membership criterion of the declared class on the same footing as the criteria for the procedure domains, and not only the conjunction of the domain: a model whose $\operatorname{Gen}_{R}$ or $\operatorname{Init}_{R}$ diverges from the field of §11 at even one argument arising from a record that passed the structural checks does not belong to the declared class. A domain conjunct alone does not give this: an incomplete procedure would carry its inputs out of the domain, and the contract would be satisfied by silence exactly where completeness fails, the observable consequence of incompleteness being a narrowing of the domain rather than a failure of the contract. ## 18. Closure

I see everything around me as an infinite nesting of systems.

Everything is structure — the founding axiom of this work. Every distinguishable structure is a system nested in other systems and containing internal structure whose distinguishable nodes are capable of acting as systems. The observer participates in this nestedness through attention, body, language and every distinction performed.

Worldlessness lies deeper than emptiness. Emptiness has already received the status of emptiness; matter identified with it has already received a position; structures that arose from its metamorphoses already belong to the unfolded world. Worldlessness means the absence of anything and therefore remains beyond the limit of object description.

The first sign that a formal language is able to hold at this boundary is $\mathbf 0_W$, Operator Zero of Will. It is a pure predicate over an entire trajectory in its own projection; §2 declares it the sole invariant under the complete stripping of shells, and separately declares its well-definedness relative to the declared class of rewritings of the pair $(P_W,\rho_W)$.

Between Zero and the Unit there exists $\mathfrak F_W^{0\rightsquigarrow1}$ — the common field of pre-unfolding. Its operator-compatible layer $\mathfrak F_{\mathrm I,i}^{[\ell,R]}$ is the field that forms the I of a concrete identity and, through the restriction $\iota_\ell^{[R]}$, projects into $\mathbf 1_i^{[\ell]}$. The Unit is the identity of a line; $\mathcal I_i^{[\ell]}=\langle\mathbf 1_i^{[\ell]},\gamma_i^{[\ell]}\rangle$ conducts it through shell history.

$\mathcal E_1$ opens the space of states of the first shell. The first performed distinction already belongs to it. The observable world begins at this step, and later lines unfold in $\mathcal E_{+}$. They carry projections of Operator Zero in their fields of the I, Units, boundaries, transformations, metamorphoses and continuations.

ONTOΣ follows these traces from shells through identity and field to Operator Zero. ONTO opens the ontological register. Σ preserves the altered inscription of Will and gradually leads the reader to the object of the series.

Admissible continuations within the declared candidate generator describe the openness of a line; realised ones its actual branching or closure. Under a unique realised continuer, metamorphosis of form preserves the line only when $K_\ell=1$ and $\operatorname{IdPres}_{\ell}^{[R]}=1$; when $\operatorname{IdPres}_{\ell}^{[R]}=0$ a new Unit arises, branching creates a family of new Units whatever the value of the predicate, and $\operatorname{Col}_{\mathrm{lin}}$ closes former lines. Collapse of the admissible interior and Regime W remain separate types.

The terminal and transmitting positions, as well as the continuation fields of the holding position, belong to executable shell trajectories. Coherence of $P_3$ is fixed by the line being active and by a positive contribution of the coordinate $\mathrm{hold}_d$ in the current window. Metamorphosis of a nested entity is capable of closing some modes of continuation and opening others; the provenance map links new Units to preceding identities.

Every part enters a whole. Neighbouring branches meet inside a common ancestor. A typed change of state of a carrier under $\operatorname{StateChg}_{R,\ell}(i,t+1)=1$ enters the realised world of every whole in the transitive closure of its nestedness: the composition coordinate — under self-continuation and closure, and for a fresh descendant and under branching — through the predicate and the map $\operatorname{state}_R$. Narrowing of the field of joint continuations of those wholes is established separately, by exhibiting a witness $\pi_{\mathrm{sys}}^\star$.

Monotone consumption of each participating carrier with a declared coordinate $\mathrm{mon}$ remains positive and does not replace a witness of change of state. Metamorphosis or $\operatorname{Col}_{\mathrm{lin}}$ of a nested identity occupies another cost coordinate. The witness $\pi_{\mathrm{sys}}^\star$ strictly narrows the predecessor-relative retained field; the topological witness $[\eta]$ is counted separately. The new active field is constructed from the resulting composition and is capable of expanding. Equality of retained fields does not cancel change of the carrier state under $\operatorname{StateChg}_{R,\ell}(i,t+1)=1$.

The surrounding world exists through mutual nestedness. We continue within it, and it continues through the composition and metamorphoses of all its carriers.

At the level of forms, active open lines form a multitude of local crowns in their connectivity classes $\ell$. Their common architectural type is the Unit; their common ground is Operator Zero.

Within declared types, models $R$ and classes of admissible trajectories, this architecture constructs the transition from operator-compatible distinction to Unit-as-identity and fixes the conditions under which declared positions of meaning become model-localisable in a shell trajectory. Empirical reading of this transition is restricted to subclasses supported by data. This is the deepest level of ground available to the present signature; further deepening remains open. The final line is an ontological compression of this architecture; the expression "change of a carrier" in it is read as $\operatorname{StateChg}_{R,\ell}(i,t+1)=1$, and not as a positive cost alone, while "narrows the field of continuations" is read as established by exhibiting a witness $\pi_{\mathrm{sys}}^\star$ and not as a consequence of the change of composition. The line remains inside the declared types, models and classes of trajectories.

**The Operator is zero. The crown of evolution is the Unit: identity arising as its projection. The field between zero and the Unit forms the I; each preserved line unfolds its Unit in its own domain, and change of any carrier enters the composition of the entire nested world linked to it through common wholes, and narrows the field of retained continuations of that whole for which a witness is exhibited.**
